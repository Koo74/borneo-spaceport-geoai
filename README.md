## 🎯 Why Borneo for a Spaceport?

Borneo is one of the **best equatorial launch locations** in the world:

| Factor | Advantage |
|---|---|
| **Latitude** | 1-7°N — near equator → Earth's rotation boost = **~460 m/s free velocity** |
| **Coastal Access** | Long coastline → safe launch trajectories over ocean |
| **Sparse Population** | Interior regions have very low density → large safety zones |
| **Established Infrastructure** | International airports, ports, and roads already exist |
| **Political Stability** | Malaysia is politically stable with strong rule of law |
| **Strategic Location** | Between Southeast Asia, Australia, and the Pacific |

**Near-equatorial launches save 20-30% fuel** compared to mid-latitude sites like Cape Canaveral (28.5°N) or Baikonur (45.6°N).
